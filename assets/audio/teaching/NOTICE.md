# Teaching phoneme audio

These WAV files are original project assets generated with the macOS British
English voice `Daniel` on 2026-08-20. No British Council recording was copied.

The six plosive teaching cues include a short schwa for clarity. This follows
the teaching convention documented by the British Council Sounds Right chart.
The eight diphthong cues are continuous glides without a surrounding example
word.

The `/θ/`, `/ʃ/`, `/tʃ/`, and `/dʒ/` WAV files contain only the consonant
portion of the source recording, with the surrounding vowel removed:

- `theta.wav`: [Voiceless Dental Fricative.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Voiceless_Dental_Fricative.ogg), HuiXingMusic, CC0 1.0.
- `sh.wav`: [Voiceless palato-alveolar sibilant.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Voiceless_palato-alveolar_sibilant.ogg), Peter Isotalo, CC BY-SA 3.0.
- `aff-ch.wav`: [Voiceless palato-alveolar affricate.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Voiceless_palato-alveolar_affricate.ogg), Peter Isotalo, CC BY-SA 3.0.
- `aff-j.wav`: [Voiced palato-alveolar affricate.ogg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Voiced_palato-alveolar_affricate.ogg), Octane, public domain.

The four derivative WAV files were cropped, normalized, and given a short fade
to remove clicks. Files derived from CC BY-SA 3.0 recordings remain available
under CC BY-SA 3.0.

The following consonant-only WAV files are cropped derivatives of the matching
recordings in `../phonemes/`. Their surrounding vowel portions were removed so
they do not sound like "fa", "sa", "ma", and similar syllables:

- `pure-f.wav`, `pure-v.wav`, `pure-eth.wav`, `pure-s.wav`, `pure-z.wav`
- `pure-zh.wav`, `pure-h.wav`, `pure-m.wav`, `pure-n.wav`, `pure-eng.wav`
- `pure-l.wav`, `pure-r.wav`, `pure-w.wav`, `pure-y.wav`

The original recordings and their CC BY-SA 3.0 attribution are documented in
`../phonemes/NOTICE.md`. These derivative WAV files remain available under CC
BY-SA 3.0.
